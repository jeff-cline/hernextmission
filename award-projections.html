Applicant & Venture Overview

A Woman-Veteran-Led Venture With Momentum

Founded and led by retired Air Force Senior Master Sergeant Krystalore Crews, Her Next Mission Inc. is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit helping female Veterans and first responders reclaim their identity, rebuild confidence, and discover their next mission — through coaching, wellness, leadership development, entrepreneurship education, retreats, and community.

Innovation

A women-centered, whole-person model — uniting somatic coaching, fitness, leadership, and entrepreneurship — organized by the proprietary NEXT Framework™. Few Veteran organizations address identity and wellbeing rather than employment alone.

Resilience

Built by a 22-year Veteran, cancer survivor, and best-selling author with an existing audience, proven programs, and a national speaking platform.

Impact

Year-1 targets: 250 women served, 25 scholarships, 75 coaching participants, and the inaugural Her Next Mission Summit.

How a $5,000 Award Would Be Used

A Melissa Washington Small Business Award would directly fund scholarships — sponsoring coaching sessions ($50 each) and retreat scholarship seats ($500 each) for women who could not otherwise participate — putting 100% of the award into direct service for women Veterans and first responders.

Her Next Mission Inc. is an early-stage 501(c)(3); these projections are submitted in lieu of historical financial statements, consistent with the award's provision for startups.

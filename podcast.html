What we cover

Five themes. Real conversations.

  • Transition. The 365 days before separation and the 365 days after — what no one prepared us for.
  • Wellness. Nervous system, sleep, somatic work, healing the body the uniform asked you to override.
  • Business. Launching a service, consultancy, or 501(c)(3) on the other side of the uniform.
  • Leadership. Taking what you learned commanding a team and leading yourself, your family, your second chapter.
  • Retreats & community. Why women heal in proximity to other women who have stood where they stand.

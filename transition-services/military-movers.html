MILITARY MOVERS

PCS, final move, and the difference between a packed-by-government move and one you self-procure.

DPS, weight allowances, and the broken claims process.

The Defense Personal Property System (DPS) routes most military moves. Your weight allowance is set by rank and dependents. Damage claims go through the carrier first, then the military. The system is improving (GHC contract) but still trips women up — especially solo movers and dual-military couples on offset orders.

Personally Procured Move (PPM, formerly DITY).

  • You move yourself; the government reimburses up to ~95% of what they would have paid the contracted mover.
  • Often the better deal if you have weight you'd lose otherwise — or if you don't trust the moving carriers in your channel.
  • Requires empty/full weigh-tickets, receipts, and timely paperwork.
  • Final move (separation) PPM rules differ from PCS — read the JTR.

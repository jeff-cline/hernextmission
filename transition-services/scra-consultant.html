SERVICEMEMBERS CIVIL RELIEF ACT (SCRA) CONSULTANT

What SCRA actually protects, when to invoke it, and how to find a consultant who'll do the paperwork right the first time.

SCRA at a glance.

  • 6% interest rate cap on pre-service debts (credit cards, mortgages, student loans, auto loans).
  • Residential lease termination when PCS or deployment 90+ days.
  • Foreclosure protection on mortgages incurred pre-service.
  • Default-judgment protection in civil court — your appearance can be stayed.
  • Eviction protection on residential leases below the rent threshold.

Where the system breaks.

Banks 'forget' the rate cap. Landlords contest the lease termination. Civil-court clerks ignore the stay request. Even VA-friendly lenders sometimes refuse SCRA benefits to dependents using the active-duty rate. A specialist consultant — usually a paralegal or military-financial counselor — gets the protections actually applied.

When to ask.

If you're being charged 18% on a card, served at home with a civil suit, or staring at a lease you can't break — yes, SCRA applies. Email us and we'll connect you to a consultant who's done the work for women in the same position.

From Service to Success Summit

A flagship gathering of women who served and women now serving themselves — Veterans, first responders, sponsors, and the leaders who fund the work. In-person, virtual, and hybrid tracks. Tiered tickets including scholarship seats so service-status never gates access. Date and venue announced soon.

  • Keynotes from women who built second careers after the uniform
  • Working sessions on transition, business launch, leadership, and somatic healing
  • Sponsor activation tracks for corporate partners
  • Press & media room for Veteran storytelling

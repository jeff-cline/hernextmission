Mentorship · 1:1

VETERAN MENTOR PROGRAMS

1:1 pairing with women who've already walked it — Veterans 3–10+ years out, building the second careers you're imagining.

She's been where you are. She isn't anymore.

Coaching is for skill. Therapy is for healing. Mentorship is for proof — proof that another woman with your backstory built the thing you're trying to build. Our mentors are Veterans 3–10+ years out, working in fields you might be heading toward: business, government, nonprofit, healthcare, law, entrepreneurship.

Pairing & commitment.

  • 6-month commitment, monthly 60-minute calls, ad-hoc text/email.
  • Pairings made by hand — not algorithm — based on field, branch, and life stage.
  • Mentor & mentee both vetted: NDAs in place, code of conduct signed.
  • Optional re-pairing at month three if the chemistry isn't right.

Become a mentor.

If you're a woman Veteran 3+ years out and you remember the year nobody saw you — we'd love you on the mentor roster. Light commitment, hand-picked match, and a private mentor-only community.

Where this fits

Inside HER NEXT MISSION's coaching system

This isn't a la carte. It's part of a connected system — coaching, retreats, and community that don't fall apart when the uniform comes off.

Mentorship · 1:1

VETERAN MENTOR PROGRAMS

1:1 pairing with women who've already walked it — Veterans 3–10+ years out, building the second careers you're imagining.

She's been where you are. She isn't anymore.

Coaching is for skill. Therapy is for healing. Mentorship is for proof — proof that another woman with your backstory built the thing you're trying to build. Our Veteran mentor programs pair you with women 3–10+ years out, working in fields you might be heading toward: business, government, nonprofit, healthcare, law, entrepreneurship, defense industry.

How pairings are made.

By hand — not by algorithm. We match on three axes:

  • Field overlap. Where you're trying to go, she's been.
  • Branch / agency overlap. Shared service culture means less translation.
  • Life stage. Caregiver-to-caregiver, founder-to-founder, single-to-single.

We pair after a 30-minute intake on each side. Re-pairing offered at month 3 if chemistry isn't right — no awkwardness.

Commitment & cadence.

  • 6-month minimum commitment, monthly 60-minute calls.
  • Ad-hoc text/email between calls — within mentor bandwidth.
  • Mentor & mentee both vetted: NDAs in place, code of conduct signed.
  • Optional cohort "mentor circles" — 1 mentor, 4 mentees, quarterly.
  • Light, ongoing community thread for all program participants.

Become a mentor.

If you're a woman Veteran 3+ years out and you remember the year nobody saw you — we'd love you on the mentor roster. Light commitment (1 mentee, 1 hour/month minimum), hand-picked match, and a private mentor-only community of women paying it forward. Volunteer to mentor →

Frequently asked

Common questions.

How long does the program last?

6 months minimum, with most pairings continuing 12+ months informally. Plenty go on for years.

How are pairings made?

By hand. Each side has a 30-minute intake call. We match on field, branch/agency, and life stage. Re-pairing offered at month 3.

What industries are mentors in?

Currently: business operations, federal civil service, healthcare administration, law, nonprofit leadership, entrepreneurship, defense industry consulting, technology. Roster grows quarterly.

Can I become a mentor?

Yes — if you're 3+ years out and you remember being where the women you'd mentor are now. Email us.

What if my mentor isn't a fit?

Re-pairing offered at month 3, no questions asked, no awkwardness. We'd rather get it right than save the original match.

Is mentorship a substitute for coaching?

No. Mentorship is proof and access. Coaching is skill-building. Many women do both. See military career coaching.

Are there scholarships?

Mentor program runs at low or no cost to mentees regardless. Scholarships fund the operations side.

PURPOSE WORKSHOPS

Cohort workshops for the question every woman asks the day after the uniform comes off: what now?

The first question after the last formation.

What now? Most transitioning women answer it alone, between two cups of coffee and a stack of half-applied job postings. Purpose Workshops are 4–8 week cohorts where she answers it in the company of women asking the same question. The work is structured. The container is brave. The output is a written, actionable next-mission plan — not a vibe.

The 8-week curriculum.

  1. Week 1 — Identity audit. Who you've been. What service made you. What you're carrying.
  2. Week 2 — Values clarification. The non-negotiables. What you'll never trade for a paycheck.
  3. Week 3 — Skills inventory. Translated into civilian language. With proof.
  4. Week 4 — Body & nervous system. Somatic work, sleep, recovery. Foundations the rest sits on.
  5. Week 5 — Possibility mapping. 5–10 directions. Not narrowed yet.
  6. Week 6 — Decision frameworks. OODA, premortem, pros/cons-with-feelings.
  7. Week 7 — The next-mission plan. Written. Specific. Yours.
  8. Week 8 — Activation. First three moves. First conversation. First commitment.

What you walk away with.

  • A written purpose statement you actually believe.
  • 3–5 concrete next moves for the next 90 days.
  • Three sister-relationships from your cohort — built to last beyond the program.
  • A nervous-system practice you can keep without an instructor.
  • A clear "not now" list — the things you can stop carrying.

Format & commitment.

  • Small cohorts of 8–12 women.
  • Weekly 90-minute live sessions on Zoom (recordings provided).
  • Structured between-session practices (~2 hours/week).
  • Private group thread for the cohort (Slack or Signal).
  • Optional in-person retreat finale at our annual Summit.
  • Scholarships available — cost is never the gate.
Frequently asked

Common questions.

What's the time commitment?

~3–4 hours per week for 8 weeks. One 90-minute live session, one short between-session practice, one cohort check-in.

How is this different from individual coaching?

Cohort work moves at the speed of the group, not the individual. The trade-off is: less customization, more proof. Watching 11 other women answer the same question changes what you believe is possible. Many women do both — workshop first, then 1:1 career coaching after.

Can my spouse join?

Not in this cohort — the container is for women who served. Spouse-focused programming is on the roadmap. Email us if it's relevant and we'll route accordingly.

What if I miss a session?

Recordings are provided. We've never had a woman drop out for missing one. Two in a row, we'll talk.

Do you offer scholarships?

Yes. Cost is never the gate. Email us with your situation.

When does the next cohort run?

We launch new cohorts quarterly. Email us to get on the list for the next start date.

Is this clinical?

No. Purpose Workshops are coaching and peer-led identity work — not therapy. If clinical care is needed we refer to providers who specialize in women Veterans. See our PTSD information page.

PURPOSE WORKSHOPS

Cohort workshops for the question every woman asks the day after the uniform comes off: what now?

The first question after the last formation.

What now? Most transitioning women answer it alone. Purpose Workshops are 4–8 week cohorts where she answers it in the company of women who are asking the same question. The work is structured. The container is brave. The output is a written, actionable next-mission plan — not a vibe.

What you walk away with.

  • A written purpose statement you actually believe.
  • 3–5 concrete next moves for the next 90 days.
  • Three sister-relationships from your cohort — built to last.
  • A nervous-system practice you can keep.
  • A clear "not now" list — the things you can stop carrying.

Format.

Small cohorts (8–12 women). Weekly 90-minute live sessions on Zoom. Structured between-session practices. A private group thread for the cohort. Optional in-person retreat finale at our annual Summit.

Where this fits

Inside HER NEXT MISSION's coaching system

This isn't a la carte. It's part of a connected system — coaching, retreats, and community that don't fall apart when the uniform comes off.

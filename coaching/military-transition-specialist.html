MILITARY TRANSITION SPECIALIST

Not a coach — a specialist. Someone who knows TAP, SkillBridge, VR&E, GI Bill, and VA disability inside and out, and gets you through the paperwork without losing months.

Specialist, not coach.

Coaches help you decide. Specialists help you execute. Our transition specialists are the operators who know which form, which window, which deadline, which benefit, and which order to do them in. They handle TAP, SkillBridge placement, VR&E (Chapter 31), Post-9/11 GI Bill transfer, and VA disability prep so you don't lose 18 months learning the system.

What we cover.

  • TAP capstone — what to actually walk in with vs. paper through.
  • SkillBridge — eligible roles, command authorization, the 180-day clock.
  • VR&E (Chapter 31) — when to choose it over the GI Bill.
  • VA disability — claim prep, evidence, BDD, the C&P exam.
  • State Veteran benefits — varies wildly; we map your destination state.

Plays well with coaching.

Most women come for tactics, stay for the coaching. Specialist work is paired with the coaching system so the paperwork wins also build the identity work — not just the W-2.

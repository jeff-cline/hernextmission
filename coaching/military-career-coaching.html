MILITARY CAREER COACHING

1-on-1 coaching for women translating a military career into a civilian one — without losing what made them who they are.

Translation, not reinvention.

Civilian recruiters don't read MOSs. They read outcomes. Most career coaches don't read either. We do both. Our work translates 22 years of leadership, ops, and accountability into language hiring committees actually understand — without sanding off the parts of you that made it possible.

What the engagement looks like.

  • Discovery (Week 1). Career assessment, identity audit, what you actually want vs. what you've been told you should want.
  • Translation (Weeks 2–4). Resume, LinkedIn, cover letter, elevator pitch, federal vs. private-sector tracks.
  • Market entry (Weeks 5–8). Interview prep, salary negotiation, network activation, decision frameworks.
  • First 90 days (post-hire). Onboarding coaching so the offer becomes the right job.

Why women specifically.

Generic Veteran career coaching is built for the average male transitioner. Women Veterans face higher unemployment in the first 12 months out, lower confidence in salary negotiation, and a labor market that under-credits their command experience. We coach the whole picture: career, identity, nervous system, family bandwidth.

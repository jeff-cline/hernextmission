Senior · Advisory

MILITARY CONSULTANT

For senior officers and senior NCOs entering corporate consulting, government affairs, defense industry, and board work — advisory built around your last 10 years of command, not your first job out.

Different market. Different help.

You're not looking for a job. You're looking to monetize 20+ years of senior responsibility. The work isn't resume coaching — it's market positioning, personal-brand build, network activation, board readiness, and pricing your experience like the asset it is.

The advisory engagement.

  • Personal positioning — who you are in the market, in one paragraph and one tagline.
  • Niche selection — corporate consulting vs. govt affairs vs. defense industry vs. board service vs. operator-CEO.
  • Thought leadership — keynote, op-ed, podcast, LinkedIn presence with cadence.
  • Network mapping — the 30 people who unlock the next decade.
  • Pricing — what your hour is worth and how to ask for it.

Who this is for.

O-3 through O-6, senior NCOs (E-7+), and Federal SES equivalents. Women retiring with command-level experience and a long resume that nobody outside the gate knows how to read. Limited slots — engagements run 90-day sprints with optional renewals.

Where this fits

Inside HER NEXT MISSION's coaching system

This isn't a la carte. It's part of a connected system — coaching, retreats, and community that don't fall apart when the uniform comes off.

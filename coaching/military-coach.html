Coaching · Frameworks

MILITARY COACH

Performance and leadership coaching that uses the frameworks you already speak: mission, OODA, after-action review, principles of war, intent.

A coach who speaks the language.

You don't need a coach who learned about leadership from a book. You need one who's run a brief, taken a counseling, written an OPORD, and watched a plan survive contact with the enemy. Our military coaches use the frameworks you already trust — mission analysis, OODA, AAR, commander's intent — to drive civilian outcomes.

What we work on.

  • Mission clarity. What are you actually trying to accomplish — civilian-life version.
  • Decision-making under uncertainty. OODA in board rooms, kitchens, and family councils.
  • After-action. Real AAR cadence on the work, the marriage, the parenting, the body.
  • Leading without rank. Influence in flat civilian orgs.
  • Recovery. Operational tempo for a life that's no longer 24/7.

1:1 or team.

Individual engagements run 90 days minimum. Team engagements available for Veteran-owned and Veteran-led businesses building leadership cohorts.

Where this fits

Inside HER NEXT MISSION's coaching system

This isn't a la carte. It's part of a connected system — coaching, retreats, and community that don't fall apart when the uniform comes off.

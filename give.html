MOVE HER MISSION FORWARD.

Every gift moves a woman through her transition — coaching, retreats, scholarships to the Summit. Recurring giving is the most impactful way to support these women.

Where your gift goes

  • Coaching scholarships. 1-on-1 sessions for women who otherwise couldn't access them.
  • Bootcamp seats. A full cohort experience for a woman in transition.
  • Retreats. Sending a woman to a multi-day immersive retreat, fully covered.
  • Regional micro-Summits. Bringing the work into your city for the women already there.
  • Named gifts. Funding an entire program track or live event in someone's honor.

Ways to give

  • Recurring monthly giving (most impactful)
  • One-time gift
  • Donor-advised fund (DAF) grant
  • Corporate matching gift
  • Stock or appreciated-asset transfer
  • Estate / planned giving

Privacy

YOUR INFORMATION, HANDLED WITH CARE.

Plain English about what we collect, how we use it, and how to remove it.

HER NEXT MISSION collects only the information you submit through forms and email handoffs (such as name, email, phone, and company for sponsor deck requests). This information is stored on our servers and shared only with team members directly responsible for your inquiry.

We never sell donor or beneficiary data. Donations are processed through PCI-compliant providers; we do not store payment-card numbers.

You can request export or deletion of your data at any time by emailing krystalore@thecrewscoach.com.
